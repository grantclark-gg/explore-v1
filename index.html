How to get the best results

Four simple habits. Better answers.

Explore works best when your question is specific. These four practices make it clear exactly what you want to see.

  1. 01

    Define the period.

    Tell Explore exactly which dates you mean.

    Instead of“Show our donations.”

    Try“Show our donations from January 1 to March 31, 2026.”

  2. 02

    Specify currency when relevant.

    If you receive donations in more than one currency, say which one you want.

    Instead of“What did we raise last year?”

    Try“What did we raise in USD in 2025?”

  3. 03

    Be clear about what you are counting.

    Donations, donors and fundraisers are different things – name the one you mean.

    Instead of“How many supporters do we have?”

    Try“How many unique donors gave to us this year?”

  4. 04

    Tell Explore how to present it.

    Ask for a table, chart, KPI summary or trend, depending on how you’ll use it.

    Instead of“Show fundraiser results.”

    Try“Show our top 10 fundraisers as a table.”

Good to know

Questions charities ask about Explore.

What is Explore?

Explore is a new way to understand your fundraising on GivenGain. Ask questions in everyday language and get clear reporting from your own GivenGain data – from quick KPI summaries to charts, trends and detailed tables.

Who can use Explore, and where do I find it?

Explore is available to every charity on GivenGain. Charity administrators can find it in the Charity Dashboard, under Reports → Explore.

Does Explore replace Donation and Payout Statements?

No. Donation and Payout Statements remain the foundation of your reporting on GivenGain. Use Statements when you need your standardized reporting, and Explore when you want to investigate, compare, analyze or create reporting around a specific question.

What kind of reporting can Explore create?

KPI summaries, written summaries, detailed tables, bar charts, pie charts and trend lines. You can also ask for event and project comparisons, donor and fundraiser analysis, and donation-to-payout reporting.

Where does Explore get its information?

Explore works only from your organization’s GivenGain reporting data. It doesn’t search the internet for financial information.

How do I know the answers are accurate?

Every answer is based on your own GivenGain reporting data, and Explore shows the detailed figures behind it so you can check the numbers yourself. The more specific your question, the more precise the answer. For your standardized reporting, continue to use your Donation and Payout Statements.

How do I get the best results?

Define the period, specify the currency when relevant, be clear about what you are counting, and tell Explore how you want the reporting presented. See How to get the best results for examples.

Is my charity’s data private and secure?

Yes. Your data is analyzed separately for your organization, is never shared with other organizations and is never used to train public AI models.

Is Explore powered by AI?

Yes. Explore uses AI to understand your question and create the reporting you ask for. But the answers always come from your own GivenGain reporting data – the point is not the technology, it’s having more flexibility in how you understand and use your fundraising data.

Do I need Excel or database skills?

No. If you can describe what you want to know, you can use Explore. There are no formulas or queries to write.

Can I export what Explore creates?

Yes. Charts can be exported as PNG or SVG files, and tables can be copied or exported as CSV files.
